Amazon Kindle Setup Guide

  1. Find your Send-to-Kindle Email:

    Go to Amazon Manage Your Content & Devices > Preferences tab > scroll down to Personal Document Settings.

  2. Authorize your Sender Email:

    In the same Personal Document Settings section, scroll down to Approved Personal Document E-mail List and click Add a new approved e-mail address.

    Add the exact email address you set as your Approved Sender Email.

  3. Choose your Email Provider:

    We recommend Resend.com (free 3,000 emails/month, easy API key), SendGrid, Brevo, or your own self-hosted homelab relay.

How it Works

  • Reader Mode Engine: Extracts clean article headlines, byline, body, and images while stripping ads and popups.
  • EPUB 3 Standard: Compiles a properly formatted ebook with navigation, metadata, and e-ink friendly styles.
  • Direct Cloud Delivery: Dispatches directly to Amazon's Whispernet service so it appears in your Kindle library within seconds.
